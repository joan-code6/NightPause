Night Pause

Fall asleep to a podcast. Your Wear OS watch notices, and your phone pauses playback.

Night Pause links sleep detection on a supported Wear OS watch to media controls on your Android phone. Choose a pause delay, enable detection on the watch, and playback pauses after you drift off. It never resumes by itself.
