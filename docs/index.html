Night Pause

Fall asleep to a podcast. Your Wear OS watch notices, and your phone pauses playback.

Choose a pause delay, enable sleep detection on the watch, and Night Pause pauses active media after you drift off. Playback does not restart by itself.

Requires an Android phone and a Wear OS watch that supports sleep detection. The phone can pause media apps that expose an Android media session.
