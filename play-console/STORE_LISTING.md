# Google Play listing draft

## App name

Night Pause

## Short description

Pause phone media after your Wear OS watch detects that you’ve fallen asleep.

## Full description

Night Pause pauses active media on your Android phone when your Wear OS watch detects that you’ve fallen asleep.

Connect your watch, enable sleep detection, allow media control on your phone, and choose how long Night Pause waits before pausing playback. It does not restart playback when you wake up.

Night Pause needs a compatible Wear OS watch with sleep-state detection support and a connected Android phone. Sleep detection availability varies by watch. The phone can pause media apps that expose an Android media session.

Night Pause is a playback convenience. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or monitor a medical condition.
